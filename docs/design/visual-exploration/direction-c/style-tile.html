Direction C — Weave · نسيج

شارك means to participate. Collaboration as weaving: individual threads — contributors, commits, skills — interlacing into shared fabric. A bilingual-first identity rooted in Arabic visual culture, executed flat and contemporary: pattern, never gradient-glow.

Brand

The logo already is two figures interweaving — this direction is the only one that builds the whole language out of the mark’s own idea (and its actual indigo + teal palette). Logo untouched; the woven band is the recurring brand element.

Primary brand expression

Art-directed dual-script headlines (Arabic leads, Latin partners — never stacked translations), a thin woven band marking brand moments, and geometric thread illustrations that explain (weaving = collaborating), never decorate randomly.

Background treatment

Warm sand field #F7F1E6; indigo surfaces for emphasis blocks. Texture comes from pattern, not shaders — no WebGL, no glassmorphism. Dark mode: indigo-night field with sand text.

The ع ⇄ EN toggle is celebrated in the header as a brand moment — not buried in settings.

Foundations

Richer palette than Registry, still flat: indigo carries structure, saffron carries progress/attention, oasis teal carries verification, madder carries negatives — mapped onto the shared status grammar. Violet stays reserved for AI processing.
