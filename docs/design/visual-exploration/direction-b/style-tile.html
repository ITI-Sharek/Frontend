Direction B — Workbench · الورشة

Share-k as excellent developer tooling. Dark slate panels, syntax-highlighting accents, monospace labels, keyboard-first affordances. Feels closer to Linear/VS Code than to a marketing site — the AI's evidence and traces look native here.

brand

The logo keeps its original colors on a white plate (never recolored for dark mode — the mark is the one full-color object). Wordmark sets in Geist Mono, reading like a package name.
